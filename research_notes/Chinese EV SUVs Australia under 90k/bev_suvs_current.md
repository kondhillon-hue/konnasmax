## Q1: Prices and the most powerful trim under $90k

### Takeaway
Four China-built SUVs pass 400kW under $90k: the MG IM6 Performance (572kW, ~$81k DA) has the most power, and the Zeekr 7X Performance and Smart #5 Brabus (475kW, ~$77–80k DA) follow. The Polestar 4 Dual Motor (400kW) only fits the budget without its option packs. For value, the BYD Sealion 7 Performance (390kW, ~$64k BORC) and Xpeng G6 AWD Performance (358kW, $63.8k BORC) lead.

## Q2: Battery, charging, range

### Takeaway
The 800V cars (Zeekr 7X, Smart #5, Xpeng G6) charge far faster (400kW+ peak, roughly 11–18 min for 10-80%) than the Sealion 7, which uses a 400V-class Blade LFP pack at 150kW. The Polestar 4 has the longest WLTP range among the high-power trims (590km).

### Cited Findings
- 7X Long Range and Performance use a 100kWh NCM battery. The 100kWh models peak at 420kW DC, the RWD at up to 450kW, and Zeekr claims 10-80% in as little as 11 min. AU cars use 800V, not the 900V setup in the newer Chinese-market 7X — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/2026-zeekr-7x-price-and-specs), [EV Central](https://evcentral.com.au/cars/zeekr/7x/)
- Smart #5: 100kWh/94kWh usable, 400kW DC, 10-80% under 18 min; WLTP 540km (Brabus) and 590km (RWD) — [Drive](https://www.drive.com.au/news/2027-smart-5-price-and-specs-zeekr-7x-twin-detailed-for-australia/)
- Xpeng G6 facelift: 800V, 10-80% in 12 min, AWD 510km — [Carsauce](https://carsauce.com/news/2026-xpeng-g6-australia-price-specs-orders-open)
- Sealion 7 Performance: 456km WLTP, 150kW DC, 11kW AC — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-reviews/2026-byd-sealion-7-performance-review), [Aussiemotor](https://aussiemotor.com.au/blog/byd-sealion-7-premium-vs-performance-australia-2026-is-the-9000-upgrade-worth-it)
- MG IM6 Performance: 100kWh, 505km WLTP, tested at 24.1kWh/100km — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-reviews/2025-im6-review), [carsales](https://www.carsales.com.au/research/mg/im6/)
- Polestar 4 Dual Motor: 94kWh, 590km, 11kW AC — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/2026-polestar-4-price-and-specs)
- Avatr 07L will use an 800V SiC platform with CATL batteries; AU specs are still to be confirmed — [The Beep](https://thebeep.com.au/car-news/avatr-07l-electric-suv-australia/)

### Inferences
- At a measured 24.1kWh/100km, the IM6's 100kWh pack would give roughly 400km real-world. The 7X's measured 18.9kWh/100km suggests 500km+ real-world, which makes the 7X the more efficient high-power choice. This is a rough calculation that assumes about 100kWh usable.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed from sources: IM6 voltage and DC rate, Polestar 4 DC rate and 10-80% time, Sealion 7 10-80% time, V2L/V2G per model, and Sealion 7 Performance usable kWh (believed 82.5kWh, but unverified here).
- Real-world range tests from Australian reviewers were mostly not reachable.

## Q3: Luxury, features, ANCAP, warranty, review verdicts

### Takeaway
Reviewers rate the Zeekr 7X Performance highly for luxury, build quality and tech for the price, and it is the only candidate here confirmed with air suspension. The MG IM6 wins on raw performance per dollar and gets a "plush interior". The Smart #5 Brabus is more powerful than its hardware suggests, since it lacks chassis upgrades over the RWD.

### Cited Findings
- Chasing Cars on the 7X Performance: "a fabulous job of delivering tangible luxury, terrific build quality and cutting-edge technology for a surprisingly sharp price"; it is the most popular 7X variant — [Chasing Cars](https://www.chasingcars.com.au/reviews/midsize-suvs/zeekr-7x-performance-awd-2026-review/)
- EV Central says the extra ~$9k over the Long Range RWD buys suspension hardware (air suspension, double wishbone/multi-link) that makes the car a more polished drive, not just more acceleration — [EV Central](https://evcentral.com.au/2026-zeekr-7x-long-range-rwd-review-is-this-actually-the-7x-to-buy/), [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/2026-zeekr-7x-price-and-specs)
- 7X: 5-star ANCAP; 5yr/unlimited-km warranty; 8yr/160,000km battery; heated front and rear seats, tri-zone climate (RWD) — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/2026-zeekr-7x-price-and-specs), [mynrma](https://www.mynrma.com.au/open-road/news/2025/zeekr-7x-specs-revealed)
- CarExpert has published a long-term conclusion review of the 7X (not read in full) — [CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-reviews/2026-zeekr-7x-review-long-term-conclusion)
- MG IM6 Performance: Exhaust Notes calls it "incredibly quick; plush interior; loaded with technology". Carsales scored it 78/100 — [Exhaust Notes](https://www.exhaustnotes.com.au/2026-mg-im6-performance-car-review/), [carsales](https://www.carsales.com.au/editorial/details/im-presented-by-mg-im6-performance-2026-review-151767/)
- Smart #5 Brabus: no changes to brakes or suspension versus the RWD — [Drive](https://www.drive.com.au/news/2027-smart-5-price-and-specs-zeekr-7x-twin-detailed-for-australia/)
- Xpeng G6 facelift comes with a longer factory warranty — [carsorted](https://www.carsorted.ai/news/latest-car-news/2026-xpeng-g6-final-pricing-orders-open-australia)

### Inferences
- For a buyer who wants big power plus luxury, the 7X Performance (air suspension, 800V, 475kW+) gives the most balanced package. The IM6 Performance suits someone who mainly wants the most kW. The Sealion 7 Performance suits buyers who want strong power at the lowest price and accept slower charging.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed per model: massage or ventilated seats, audio brands, HUD, ADAS detail, ANCAP for IM6, #5, G6 facelift, Sealion 7 Performance and Polestar 4, and warranty terms for most non-Zeekr models. No search confirmed massage seats or Dolby audio on the 7X.
- I couldn't read the WhichCar, Wheels or AutocarNZ verdicts.
