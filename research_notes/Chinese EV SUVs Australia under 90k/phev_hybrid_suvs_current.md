## Q1: Price (AUD RRP and drive-away; NZD) — especially the most powerful trim under $90k

### Takeaway
Every high-power (300 kW+) Chinese AWD PHEV SUV fits comfortably under $90k drive-away. The cheapest is the Haval H6 GT Hi4 at about $53k d/a (321 kW). The 7-seat 315-359 kW group (Tiggo 9, J8, Sealion 8 AWD, Omoda 9) sits at about $60-78k. The Tank 500 Hi4-T is the dearest at about $80k +ORC, which may land near the $90k d/a ceiling. Prices are heavily promotional and change month to month.

## Q3: Battery, EV range, total range, fuel consumption

Almost all of the high-power models carry about 34-37 kWh batteries for roughly 100-150 km of WLTP EV range (150-183 km NEDC). Real-world EV range runs about 120-130 km for the 34 kWh Chery-group cars. Once the battery is flat, hybrid-mode fuel use is around 6-7 L/100 km, far above the 1-2 L/100 km official figures.

## Q8: Australian tax context (FBT, LCT)

### Takeaway
PHEVs lost the FBT electric-car exemption from 1 April 2025 (confirmed). Battery EVs keep it, subject to Budget 2026 phase-down proposals. The LCT fuel-efficient threshold is $91,387 for 2025-26. One unverified source gives about $91,661 for 2026-27, so everything in this list sits under the LCT threshold.

### Cited Findings
- PHEVs no longer qualify for the FBT exemption from 1 April 2025. Pre-existing arrangements are grandfathered only if they are unchanged. — [Cars24](https://www.cars24.com.au/car-news/end-of-phev-tax-break-in-australia/); [smart.com.au](https://www.smart.com.au/novated-leasing/phev-exemption/)
- Budget 2026 proposes phasing down the EV FBT exemption, with a 25% discount by 1 April 2029. It does not restore PHEVs. — [BDO](https://www.bdo.com.au/en-au/insights/budget/2026/farewell-to-the-full-fbt-exemption-on-evs); [MyNRMA](https://www.mynrma.com.au/open-road/news/2026/ev-tax-breaks-to-shrink)
- LCT fuel-efficient threshold for 2025-26 is $91,387. The 2026-27 figure of $91,661 comes from one non-ATO source. — [austax.tools](https://austax.tools/tax-insights/federal-budget-2026-fbt-electric-car-changes/)

### Gaps
- The 2026-27 LCT threshold was not verified on ato.gov.au.
- Whether PHEVs still qualify for LCT fuel-efficient treatment (at or under 3.5 L/100 km) was not explicitly sourced, though all of these PHEVs are rated below that.
