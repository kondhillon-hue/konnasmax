## 2. Powertrain variants

### Takeaway
The 8X is a 900V plug-in hybrid (described in places as range-extender style) built on Zeekr's SEA-S architecture. It pairs a 2.0-litre turbo petrol engine with either two electric motors (up to 660 kW and 935 Nm) or three motors (1,030 kW and 1,410 Nm, 0–100 km/h in 2.96 s). All variants are AWD. These are China-spec figures.

### Cited Findings
- The engine is a 2.0T four-cylinder. Pre-launch leaks rated it at 275 hp, about 205 kW. [CarNewsChina, 8 Jan 2026](https://carnewschina.com/2026/01/08/zeekr-8x-plug-in-hybrid-suv-key-parameters-exposed-275-hp-2-0t-engine-70-kwh-battery-5-1-meters-length/)
- An export spec sheet lists a 205 kW engine plus a 145 kW generator. [Port Auto](https://www.portauto.com/zeekr-8x-full-specifications-trim-comparison-portauto-export/)
- Tri-motor: 1,030 kW (1,380–1,381 hp), 0–100 km/h in 2.96 s. [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/03/10/zeekr-8x-opens-pre-sales-on-march-16-with-1030-kw-tri-motor-phev-system/), [CarNewsChina launch](https://carnewschina.com/2026/04/17/zeekr-8x-launched-900v-hybrid-suv-tri-motor-1380-hp-0-100-in-2-96s-starts-45806-usd-in-china/)
- Dual-motor: 660 kW (898 PS) and 935 Nm. The tri-motor makes 1,410 Nm (system figures). [Wikipedia: Zeekr 8X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeekr_8X), [autohled](https://www.autohled.cz/en/a/zeekr/8x)
- CarNewsChina tested the "Ultra Plus" at 885 hp. [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/05/03/sunday-china-drive-zeekr-8x-ultra-plus-tested-with-885-hp-hybrid-europe-entry-late-2026/)

## 3. Battery, range and consumption

### Takeaway
The 8X offers two battery packs, 55.1 kWh and 70 kWh, reported as NMC/ternary chemistry. EV range is about 256–328 km WLTC, or up to 410 km CLTC on the top 70 kWh trims. Combined range is up to 1,416 km CLTC on twin-motor versions and 1,210 km on the tri-motor. Fuel use with the battery depleted is about 6.4–6.5 L/100 km WLTC.

## 4. Charging

### Takeaway
On a 900V system with a 6C-capable 70 kWh pack, the 8X claims 20–80% in about 9 minutes. One spec table lists a DC peak of about 500 kW, and MotorWatt gives about 10.3 minutes for 10–80%. These are China figures that depend on a suitable charger. No AU-specific AC rating or V2L data was found.

### Cited Findings
- The 900V 70 kWh pack is 6C-capable and charges 20–80% in about 9 minutes. [search summary of CnEVPost / CarNewsChina](https://cnevpost.com/2026/04/17/zeekr-launches-8x-suv/)
- MotorWatt lists DC 10–80% in about 10.3 minutes. [MotorWatt](https://ev.motorwatt.com/ev-database/database-electric-cars/zeekr-8x)
- One spec table lists peak DC power at 500 kW. [search summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeekr_8X)

### Inferences
- In Australia, few public chargers supply 900V at 400 kW or more. Expect a real-world 10–80% time of roughly 20–30 minutes on common 350 kW sites, possibly better, and much slower on 400V 50–150 kW units. This is an estimate.

### Gaps
- Confirmed DC peak for the 55 kWh pack.
- AC onboard charger kW.
- V2L/V2G output.

## 11. Australian tax: LCT and FBT

### Takeaway
As a PHEV, the 8X gets no FBT novated-lease exemption, which is confirmed. Its LCT status depends on its ADR 81/02 combined figure, which is not published. PHEVs with 55–70 kWh packs typically rate well under 3.5 L/100 km, so it would probably qualify for the $91,661 fuel-efficient threshold. That is an inference.

### Cited Findings
- Neither the 8X nor the 9X qualifies for the FBT novated-lease exemption because both are PHEVs. [search summary of Drive/CarExpert](https://www.carexpert.com.au/car-news/zeekr-8x-plug-in-hybrid-suv-locked-in-for-australia)
- The China WLTC battery-depleted figure is about 6.4–6.5 L/100 km. That is not the ADR combined figure used for LCT. [Port Auto](https://www.portauto.com/zeekr-8x-full-specifications-trim-comparison-portauto-export/)

### Inferences
- With 256–328 km of WLTC EV range, the ADR 81/02 weighted combined figure is very likely below 1–2 L/100 km. That makes the 2026–27 fuel-efficient LCT threshold ($91,661) likely to apply. A ~$85k base grade would then attract little or no LCT, while higher grades would pay 33% on the value above $91,661.
- This needs confirmation from the AU Green Vehicle Guide or ADR listing at launch.

### Gaps
- AU ADR combined consumption.
- Confirmation from Zeekr Australia of LCT treatment.
