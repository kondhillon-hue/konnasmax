## 2. Powertrains (China and AU)

### Takeaway
China has 6 variants: 4 BEV (RWD 270 kW, AWD 430 kW) and 2 EREV (AWD only). The EREV uses a 1.5L turbo petrol generator (110 kW) that does not drive the wheels, with about 370 kW / 695 Nm system output and 0–100 km/h in 4.95 s. AU powertrain specs are unconfirmed.

### Cited Findings
- 6 China variants: 4 BEV and 2 EREV, with both EREVs AWD. The entry BEV is the "702km RWD EV Max" — [CnEVPost launch](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/17/xpeng-launches-g9l-china/); [CnEVPost pre-sale](https://cnevpost.com/2026/08/11/xpeng-starts-g9l-pre-sales/)
- BEV RWD: single rear motor, 270 kW (362 hp), 0–100 in 6.8 s. BEV AWD: 430 kW (577 hp), 0–100 in 4.45 s — [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/17/xpeng-launches-g9l-china/); [evkx](https://evkx.net/models/xpeng/g9l/g9l_91.9_kwh_rwd/specifications/)
- EREV AWD: 0–100 in 4.95 s. The 1.5L turbo range extender makes 110 kW, acts as a generator only, and has no mechanical drive to the wheels — [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/17/xpeng-launches-g9l-china/); [CarNewsChina](https://carnewschina.com/2026/08/11/xpeng-g9l-revealed-price-with-rear-wheel-steering-755-km-range/)
- EREV system power is **370 kW (496 hp)** per CarNewsChina and its database, but **405 kW** per an autocango table. This is a conflict — [CarNewsChina DB (provisional)](https://data.carnewschina.com/database/xpeng/xpeng-g9l/2026/params); [autocango](https://www.autocango.com/news-detail/xpeng-g9l-launch-vla-distillation)

## 4. Charging

### Takeaway
The 800V platform supports up to 5C charging. XPeng claims 10–80% in about 11 minutes (Europe quotes 11.7 min for BEV) and 450 km added in 9 minutes. The EREV-specific peak kW and 10–80% time were not confirmed. V2L is 6 kW external plus a 2.2 kW interior socket.

### Cited Findings
- 5C charging, 10–80% in up to 11 minutes, and 450 km added in 9 minutes — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XPeng_G9L); [CnEVPost](https://cnevpost.com/2026/09/17/xpeng-launches-g9l-china/)
- Europe: "11.7-minute fast charging" — [Talk Android](https://www.talkandroid.com/533128-xpeng-g9l-europe-launch-price/)
- 91.9 kWh RWD BEV peak DC is 451 kW. The spec page was marked in progress — [evkx](https://evkx.net/models/xpeng/g9l/g9l_91.9_kwh_rwd/specifications/)
- V2L: 6 kW external via CCS2 connector and 2.2 kW interior socket (HK spec). EV Database lists 6.0 kW AC through a Type 2 adapter — [XPeng HK spec](https://xpeng.com.hk/g9l-spec); [EV Database](https://ev-database.org/car/3839/XPENG-G9L-RWD-Long-Range)

### Inferences
- In practice, few AU DC sites deliver more than 350 kW. Real-world AU charging will be capped well below 5C peak at most chargers.

### Gaps
